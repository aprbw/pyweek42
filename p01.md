```
Game Title: Grain of Doubt

Core Concept:
A fragile hourglass falling through an infinite, crumbling cosmic hourglass void. The player must dodge falling glass shards while collecting golden grains of sand to sustain time flow and descent.

Key Mechanics:
- Temporal Pressure: Sand depletion drives constant downward movement and continuous attrition.
- Kairos Moments: Critical inflection points requiring rapid decision-making.
- Faustian Bargains: High-stakes bargains offering immediate survivability or time extensions at the expense of permanent visual distortions, accelerated decay, or tighter future constraints.
```

```
Hourglass Game - Faustian Bargain Mechanics

1. Sands of Gluttony
Effect: Doubles the spawn count of both sand grains and shards.

2. Sands of Pride
Effect: Doubles game speed while doubling the point value of each score event.

3. Sands of Greed
Effect: Each sand grain or shard collected before death converts into an integer multiplier applied to the current score.
Penalty: Activates a hidden, sudden-death timer.

4. Sands of Wrath
Effect: Destroys all active and incoming sand grains and shards for a set duration (e.g., 10 seconds).
Penalty: Yields zero rewards, forcing the player to endure pure emptiness.

5. Sands of Sloth
Effect: Decelerates hazard velocity to increase reaction time.
Penalty: Permanently reduces baseline player movement speed.
```
