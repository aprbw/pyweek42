## 02. Narrative Premise & 16-Color Visual Topology

**Falling Hourglass Endless Runner:** A 2D retro arcade game where the player steers a fragile falling hourglass down the cosmic-scale neck of a broken hourglass, dodging lethal glass shards, collecting golden sand to survive, and choosing high-stakes (Faustian) bargains that pause time for instant boosts with permanent penalties.

### Core Game Specification
*   **Genre:** 2D Retro Arcade Endless Runner (hyper-casual single-axis controls, SkiFree-style infinite horizontal arena).
*   **Author:** Arian Prabowo.
*   **Theme:** "Borrowed Time" (PyWeek 42).
*   **Title:** *Grain of Doubt*.
*   **Version:** v1.1.0.
*   **Visual Motif (Hourglass-ception):** A fragile player hourglass falling through the neck of a colossal broken cosmic hourglass. This nested framing directly dictates gameplay: collecting stray cascading sand (points) while dodging lethal glass shards (broken fragments of the outer hourglass).
*   **Core Traversal Loop:** *SkiFree*-style downward descent with zero stopping and an **infinitely wide horizontal arena** (player steers laterally $x \in (-\infty, +\infty)$ with horizontal camera tracking and a $\pm 4.5$ screen procedural generation horizon).
*   **Mechanics:** Dual-Phase Descent & Bargains
    *   *Chronos time:* Normal play is collecting sands and avoiding glass shards (8.0s descent). Diegetic background void stars and collapsing cosmic hourglass walls provide dynamic temporal stimuli indicating progression toward Kairos (no HUD progress bar).
    *   *Kairos time:* Periodic time-freezes force the player to choose between exactly 2 Faustian bargains within 2.0s with a 1.0s input lockout (failure to choose causes instant death by doubt paralysis).
    *   *Faustian Bargains:* Named directly after the Seven Deadly Sins in Catholic Gregorian order (**Pride**, **Greed**, **Lust**, **Envy**, **Gluttony**, **Wrath**, **Sloth**). Every deal has an instant boon and permanent compounding curse. Chosen strictly via `A`/`Left Arrow` (Left card) or `D`/`Right Arrow` (Right card).

### What is Borrowed Time? A Thematic Exploration

**Archetypes & Real-World Parallels:** "Borrowed time" is the precarious state of continuing to function after an inevitable fatal condition has been established, surviving strictly on deferrals and compounding interest.

*   **Terminal Deferral:** Existing past an inescapable death sentence, terminal diagnosis, or structural insolvency. The subject is never saved; they are merely awaiting the postponed execution of the debt.
*   **Linear Flow vs. Opportune Ruptures (*Chronos* vs. *Kairos*):** In Greek thought, *Chronos* is the relentless, quantitative sequence of clock time devouring all creation. *Kairos* is the qualitative, opportune rupture—a fleeting opening where normal time freezes and a critical decision can alter immediate destiny. The player cannot stop Chronos; they can only steal desperate Kairos reprieves.
*   **The Sword of Damocles:** Enjoying supreme luxury and fortune while a razor-sharp sword hangs overhead by a single horsehair. Doom is certain, but the exact moment of severance is concealed.
*   **Faustian Bargain:** A pact with the Devil in exchange for temporary worldly power, youth, or knowledge. A Faustian bargain does not solve mortality—it trades long-term viability for immediate relief, living on borrowed grace while the debt accumulates.

## 03. Dual-Clock Temporal Engine (Chronos vs. Kairos)

*   **Temporal State Diagram:**
    ```
    [INIT] -> [TITLE_SCREEN] <---> [LORE_CODEX]
                   |
                   v
          [CHRONOS_DESCENT (8.0s)] <----> [KAIROS_CIRCUIT_BREAKER (2.0s)]
                   |                                   |
                   +---------> [GAME_OVER] <-----------+
    ```
*   **State 1: Chronos Phase (Relentless Hypervigilance):**
    *   Duration: Fixed 8.0 seconds ($240$ frames at 30 FPS).
    *   Continuous Descent: Player descends vertically through void. Global vertical scroll speed $V_y$ streams glass shards and golden sand motes upward relative to camera.
    *   Score Collection: Collecting golden sand grains increments accumulated score ($Score \leftarrow Score + (\text{Points} \times M_{pts})$).
    *   Lethal Threat: Colliding with falling glass shards inflicts damage ($-1$ Heart out of initial 5). Depleting all 5 hearts causes catastrophic shatter and instant Game Over.
*   **State 2: Kairos Phase (Acute Panic Circuit Breaker):**
    *   Trigger: Automatically halts Chronos every 8.0 elapsed seconds.
    *   Duration: 2.0 real-time seconds ($60$ frames at 30 FPS).
    *   Circuit Breaker Mechanics: Kinematic velocities freeze ($V_y = 0$, player motion locked). Background void dims as searing geometric cards illuminate the display.
    *   UI Modal: System presents exactly 2 randomly drawn Faustian Bargain cards from the Seven Deadly Sins pool displayed across 2 wide spatial columns (Left vs. Right).
    *   **Pro Mode 3-Line Presentation:**
        - Line 1: 2-letter abbreviation (`Pd`, `Gd`, `Lt`, `Ey`, `Gy`, `Wh`, `Sh`) in 3x huge font (`scale=6`).
        - Line 2: Sin name written in full (`scale=2`).
        - Line 3: Level number alone without the word "level" in 3x huge font (`scale=6`).
        - High-contrast text: Wrath (red card) uses pure white font for WCAG AAA contrast.
    *   **Reader Mode Justified KJV Presentation:**
        - Sin Title in scale 3.
        - Level indicator (`COVENANT {k+1}`).
        - Authentic King James Version descriptive paragraph formatted in justified alignment without bullet points.

## 04. Faustian Bargain Mechanics & Mathematical Decay Curves

*   **Seven Deadly Sins Bargain Registry (Catholic Canonical Order):**
    *   **Pride (Pd):** Boon: Spawns golden sand in organic randomized clusters (+1 grain per pact level). Penalty: Increases global descent velocity multiplier by flat $+25\%$ per pact level. Wave spawn scales with speed to preserve constant spatial hazard density.
    *   **Greed (Gd):** Boon: Score Multiplier $\times 110\%$ per sand grain. Penalty: Triggers **Borrowed Time** for 10.0 to 18.0 seconds. Void erupts into blood crimson, descent pace accelerates by $+20\% \times 1.5^k$, and hazard spawn accelerates by $+25\% \times 1.5^k$. **When Borrowed Time expires, the debt is collected and the player instantly dies.**
    *   **Lust (Lt):** Boon: Permanent sand magnet pull ($r = 100.0 + k \times 50.0$ px) with Newtonian momentum. Penalty: Permanent hazard glass magnet pull ($r = 100.0 + k \times 50.0$ px) with Newtonian momentum.
    *   **Envy (Ey):** Boon: **Tidal Pull:** 2.0s burst attracting all sand within $2 \times R_{\text{vignette}}$ ($1228.8$ px on Pact 1). Penalty: **Vignette Vision**, restricting visual range with a 5-tier graduated dither mask using recalibrated starting point $k_{\text{eff}} = k + 2$.
    *   **Gluttony (Gy):** Boon: **Fat Sand Grains:** Probability $1.0 - 0.9^N$, awarding $3$ points per grain ($10\times$ area). Penalty: **Fat Glass Shards:** Probability $1.0 - 0.9^N$ ($10\times$ area).
    *   **Wrath (Wh):** Boon: **Wrath Blast:** Generates a massive 1200px radial kinetic shockwave and screen shake. Penalty: 10.0s Zero Harvest Score Yield (non-compounding).
    *   **Sloth (Sh):** Boon: **Lazy Reprieve:** Shards below player within 3 screens wide hurled downward to bottom ($v_y \ge 38.0$ px/frame) for ~2.0s do-nothing safe pocket. Penalty: Returning double danger wave + permanent lateral steering drag $-20\% \times 1.5^k$.

## 05. Kinematic Controls, Hitboxes & Particle Physics

*   **Kinematics & Gliding:**
    *   Horizontal coordinate $x \in (-\infty, +\infty)$ with horizontal camera tracking $cam_x = int(x - 300)$.
    *   Friction $\mu_x = 0.94$, acceleration $a_x = 2.4$, terminal velocity $v_{x, max} \approx 37.6$ px/frame.
*   **Pro Mode Relative Kinematics:**
    *   Background blueprint grid moves upward at $1.6\times$ scroll speed (`dist_bg = int(dist * 1.6)`).
    *   Shards and grains moving at $1.0\times$ speed visually travel **DOWN** relative to the background ($0.6\times$ speed) while the player falls **DOWN** through the grid even faster ($1.6\times$ speed).
    *   Relative screen/camera gameplay is completely preserved, leaving all mechanics tests 100% valid.
