## 01. System Mandate & Operational Envelope

**Autonomous Execution Target:** You are an autonomous software engineering agent tasked with building, validating, and packaging the complete retro game **"Grain of Doubt"** using the Python **Pyxel** game engine for PyWeek 42.

*   **Primary Deliverable:** Functional, zero-crash Python codebase executable via `python -m pyxel run main.py`, accompanied by automated unit validation suites verifying mechanics in headless mode.
*   **Secondary Deliverable:** Standalone `.pyxapp` distribution package and static WebAssembly HTML bundle deployable to GitHub Pages.

## 02. Narrative Premise & 16-Color Visual Topology

**Falling Hourglass Endless Runner:** A 2D retro arcade game where the player steers a fragile falling hourglass down the cosmic-scale neck of a broken hourglass, dodging lethal glass shards, collecting golden sand to survive, and choosing high-stakes (Faustian) bargains that pause time for instant boosts with permanent penalties.

### Core Game Specification
*   **Genre:** 2D Retro Arcade Endless Runner (score attack with hyper-casual controls, akin to *SkiFree* and *Temple Run*).
*   **Theme:** "Borrowed Time" (PyWeek 42).
*   **Title:** *Grain of Doubt*.
*   **Visual Motif (Hourglass-ception):** A fragile player hourglass falling through the neck of a colossal broken cosmic hourglass. This nested framing directly dictates gameplay: collecting stray cascading sand (points) while dodging lethal glass shards (broken fragments of the outer hourglass).
*   **Core Traversal Loop:** *SkiFree*-style downward descent with zero stopping (constant unstoppable downward velocity; player can only steer left/right/center, never halt or reverse).
*   **Mechanics:** Dual-Phase Descent & Bargains
    *   *Chronos time:* Normal play is collecting sands and avoiding glass shards.
    *   *Kairos time:* Periodic time-freezes force the player to choose a deal (skipping is not allowed).
    *   *Faustian Bargains:* Every deal has both a pro (instant boost or score multiplier) and a permanent con (e.g. faster descent, denser hazards, or reduced vision), forcing the player to borrow safety today at the cost of tomorrow's survival. There are 7 distinct bargains in total, each named after one of the Seven Deadly Sins.

### What is Borrowed Time? A Thematic Exploration

**Archetypes & Real-World Parallels:** "Borrowed time" is the precarious state of continuing to function after an inevitable fatal condition has been established, surviving strictly on deferrals and compounding interest.

*   **Related Concepts to "Borrowed Time":**
    * *Terminal Deferral:* Existing past an inescapable death sentence, terminal diagnosis, or structural insolvency. The subject is never saved; they are merely awaiting the postponed execution of the debt.
    * *Linear Flow vs. Opportune Ruptures (*Chronos* vs. *Kairos*):* In Greek thought, *Chronos* is the relentless, quantitative sequence of clock time devouring all creation. *Kairos* is the qualitative, opportune rupture—a fleeting opening where normal time freezes and a critical decision can alter immediate destiny. The player cannot stop Chronos; they can only steal desperate Kairos reprieves.
    * *The Sword of Damocles:* Enjoying supreme luxury and fortune while a razor-sharp sword hangs overhead by a single horsehair. Doom is certain, but the exact moment of severance is concealed.
    * *Faustian Bargain:* A pact with the Devil in exchange for temporary worldly power, youth, or knowledge. A Faustian bargain does not solve mortality—it trades long-term viability for immediate relief, living on borrowed grace while the debt accumulates.

### Thematic & Mechanical Design Decisions

**Grounded Creative Rationale:** Every visual element, traversal vector, and game rule directly translates the existential premise of "Borrowed Time" into playable systems without arbitrary video game tropes.

*   **Visual Motif: Why "Hourglass-ception"?**
    *   *Diegetic Asset Ecology:* Nesting a player hourglass inside the neck of a shattered cosmic hourglass provides an organic justification for all game entities: collectible points are stray falling sand grains, while lethal obstacles are falling glass shards from the outer shell.
    *   *Cosmic Recursion:* A fragile micro-vessel tumbling down a macro-vessel visualizes existential entrapment—individual mortal existence trapped within the larger entropic collapse of universal time.

*   **Traversal Vector: Why Downward Descent with Zero Stopping?**
    *   *Physical & Metaphorical Entropy:* Traditional runners move forward toward an open horizon, suggesting progress. Sand and gravity only move downward. Descent embodies collapse and loss of control—what goes up must come down.
    *   *Unforgiving Clock:* Time never stops or rewinds. Removing the ability to halt subjects the player to relentless gravitational pull toward terminal impact, able only to steer laterally across oncoming danger.

*   **Core Mechanics: Why Time-Freezes and Mandatory Cursed Deals?**
    *   *Dual-Pacing Tension (Chronos vs. Kairos):* Continuous dodging alone is merely a reflex test. Freezing the frantic descent for a brief decision window shifts the experience from kinetic reaction to psychological agony, forcing tactical reflection amidst chaos.
    *   *Borrowed Time as Predatory Debt:* Power-ups are not free gifts. True to the theme of borrowed time, every deal is mandatory and pairs immediate survival (a pro) with permanent structural decay (a con). Like a predatory loan, taking a deal buys immediate reprieve today by mortgaging future survival.

*   **Title: Why "Grain of Doubt"?**
    *   *Literal Grains of Sand:* "Grain" refers directly to the grains of sand inside the hourglass—the discrete physical units of time and collectible score cascading down the void.
    *   *The English Idiom:* "A grain of doubt" is an established English idiom meaning even the smallest trace of uncertainty that undermines complete confidence.
    *   *Kairos Decision Agony:* In gameplay, this captures the paralyzing doubt players experience during Kairos time-freezes. When forced to choose between mandatory Faustian bargains, the player agonizes over trade-offs: *Which curse is survivable? Is the immediate boost worth future ruin?* That psychological hesitation—a single grain of doubt—is what shatters the run.

### Visual Art Style Guide & Psychological Framework

**Core Philosophy:** Aesthetic instantiates existential entrapment and borrowed time. Visual topology is governed by a two-stage psychological framework mapping emotional extremes to geometric and chromatic limits within the standard 16-color Pyxel palette.

*   **Stage 1: Chronos (Relentless Hypervigilance):**
    *   *State:* Attrition and prolonged tension. Emulates terminal illness, combat zones, or imminent eviction.
    *   *Visual Paradigm:* The void. Monopolized by deep, dark color palettes, strictly pitch-black (`Color 0`) and navy (`Color 1` / `Color 5`), establishing an oppressive, isolating cosmic abyss.
*   **Stage 2: Kairos (Acute Panic):**
    *   *State:* Impossible choices and immediate crisis. Emulates ruinous medical debt, predatory loans, or exploitative legal compromises.
    *   *Visual Paradigm:* Aggressive geometry intersecting searing, saturated highlights (`Color 7` stark white, `Color 9` bright orange, `Color 10` golden yellow, and `Color 8` warning red). The dark void is abruptly ruptured by luminous, high-contrast decision runes.
*   **16-Color Palette Allocation:**
    *   `Color 0 (Black)`: Void backdrop during Chronos descent.
    *   `Color 1 (Navy) / Color 5 (Dark Blue)`: Collapsing cosmic hourglass frame borders and celestial strata.
    *   `Color 7 (White) / Color 6 (Light Cyan)`: Reflective glass sheen, brittle hazard shards, collision sparks.
    *   `Color 9 (Orange) / Color 10 (Yellow)`: Luminescent golden sand grains, UI score readouts.
    *   `Color 8 (Red) / Color 2 (Dark Purple)`: Kairos decision runes, sudden-death warnings, critical damage indicators.
*   **Visual Atmosphere & Tunnel Vision:** Dynamic circular alpha vignette mask centered on hourglass player. When vision penalties trigger, radius $R_{vignette}$ contracts, immersing screen edges in opaque darkness.

## 03. Dual-Clock Temporal Engine (Chronos vs. Kairos)

**Bifurcated Temporal State Architecture:** The game loop rejects singular linear time, implementing an explicit state machine oscillating between continuous relentless motion (**Chronos**) and frozen decision crossroads (**Kairos**).

*   **Temporal State Diagram:**
    ```
    [INIT] -> [TITLE_SCREEN] -> [CHRONOS_DESCENT (8.0s)] <----> [KAIROS_CIRCUIT_BREAKER (2.0s)]
                                      |                                   |
                                      +---------> [GAME_OVER] <-----------+
    ```
*   **State 1: Chronos Phase (Relentless Hypervigilance):**
    *   Duration: Fixed 8.0 seconds ($240$ frames at 30 FPS).
    *   Continuous Descent: Player descends vertically through void. Global vertical scroll speed $V_y$ streams glass shards and golden sand motes upward relative to camera.
    *   Score Collection: Collecting golden sand grains increments accumulated score ($Score \leftarrow Score + (\text{Points} \times M_{pts})$).
    *   Lethal Threat: Colliding with falling glass shards inflicts damage ($-1$ Heart). Depleting all hearts causes catastrophic shatter and instant Game Over.
*   **State 2: Kairos Phase (Acute Panic Circuit Breaker):**
    *   Trigger: Automatically halts Chronos every 8.0 elapsed seconds.
    *   Duration: 2.0 real-time seconds ($60$ frames at 30 FPS).
    *   Circuit Breaker Mechanics: Kinematic velocities freeze ($V_y = 0$, player motion locked). Background void dims as searing geometric cards illuminate the display.
    *   UI Modal: System presents 3 randomly drawn Faustian Bargain cards from the Seven Deadly Sins pool labeled `[1]`, `[2]`, `[3]`.
    *   Player Input: Pressing key `1`, `2`, or `3` instantly applies the selected bargain, resumes Chronos descent, and resets the 8.0-second cycle timer. If timer reaches 2.0s without input, default penalty applies (no boon awarded, Kairos collapses).

## 04. Faustian Bargain Mechanics & Mathematical Decay Curves

**Systems Architecture (Variation A: Mechanical):** Every bargain pairs immediate survival boons with compounding structural penalties, mapping the Seven Deadly Sins to mechanical trade-offs.

*   **Seven Deadly Sins Bargain Registry (Variation A: Mechanical):**
    *   **Sands of Gluttony:**
        *   *Effect:* Global entity spawn rate (resources + hazards) scaled $\times 2.0$.
    *   **Sands of Pride:**
        *   *Effect:* Global score multiplier $\times 2.0$. Global temporal state velocity scaled $\times 2.0$.
    *   **Sands of Greed:**
        *   *Boon:* Accrued entity count applies integer multiplier to current score.
        *   *Penalty:* Initializes hidden deterministic kill-timer.
    *   **Sands of Wrath:**
        *   *Boon:* Global entity wipe (resources + hazards) for 10.0s duration.
        *   *Penalty:* Zero resource generation; enforces zero-yield state.
    *   **Sands of Sloth:**
        *   *Boon:* Hazard vertical descent velocity reduced.
        *   *Penalty:* Player baseline horizontal translation speed permanently reduced.
    *   **Sands of Envy:**
        *   *Boon:* Instant retroactive acquisition of bypassed resources.
        *   *Penalty:* Delayed, permanent resource repulsion field initialized.
    *   **Sands of Lust:**
        *   *Boon:* Temporary resource attraction radius activated.
        *   *Penalty:* Permanent hazard attraction radius activated.

*   **Compounding Penalty & Diminishing Return Equations:**
    *   Let $k_i \in \mathbb{N}_0$ represent the number of times bargain $i$ has been selected.
    *   Boon scaling: $Boon_i(k_i) = Boon_{i, 0} \times (0.75)^{k_i}$
    *   Curse scaling: $Curse_i(k_i) = Curse_{i, 0} \times (1.50)^{k_i}$
    *   *Invariant Assertion:* Re-selecting identical sins exponentially accelerates player demise.

## 05. Kinematic Controls, Hitboxes & Particle Physics

**Downhill Traversal Engine:** Motion echoes classic downhill vertical runners (SkiFree) inverted into cosmic celestial void descent.

*   **Control Mapping:**
    *   `Left Arrow` / `Key A`: Accelerate lateral left vector ($-\hat{i}$).
    *   `Right Arrow` / `Key D`: Accelerate lateral right vector ($+\hat{i}$).
    *   `Down Arrow` / `Key S`: Engage dive (increases descent speed by $+50\%$, multiplying score tick rate).
    *   `Up Arrow` / `Key W`: Apply aero-braking (decreases descent speed by $-25\%$, stabilizing precision collection).
    *   `1`, `2`, `3`: Select corresponding Faustian Bargain during Kairos state.
    *   `R`: Restart game from post-mortem screen.
*   **Kinematic State Vector:**
    *   Player Position: $\mathbf{P} = (x, y)$, clamped to display boundaries $x \in [8, W - 8]$.
    *   Horizontal Velocity: $v_x(t + 1) = (v_x(t) + a_x) \times \mu_x$, where damping friction $\mu_x = 0.82$.
*   **Entity Hitbox Topology:**
    *   Player Hourglass: Centered AABB $8 \times 12$ pixels.
    *   Sand Grains: $2 \times 2$ pixel glistening golden motes. Collection hitbox: $6 \times 6$ pixels. Effect: $+100$ score points.
    *   Glass Shards: Irregular polygon or rotated $4 \times 8$ triangular fragments. Collision hitbox: $4 \times 6$ pixels. Effect: $-1$ Life, screen shake (intensity 4px for 8 frames), 1.0s invulnerability flash.

## 06. Pyxel Engine Constraints & Audio Synthesis Matrix

**Technical Architecture & Hardware Budget:** Built strictly within the lightweight limitations of the Pyxel virtual retro console.

*   **Display Dimensions & Framerate:**
    *   Virtual Canvas: $160 \times 120$ pixels (or $256 \times 256$).
    *   Target Framerate: Fixed $30$ FPS (via `pyxel.init(width=160, height=120, title="Grain of Doubt", fps=30)`).
*   **Procedural Audio Definitions (`pyxel.sound`):**
    *   `Sound 0 (Sand Collect)`: High-pitch arpeggio ping (`c3e3g3c4`, tone="T", volume="4321", speed=4).
    *   `Sound 1 (Shard Impact)`: Noise crunch collision (`f2d2b1g1`, tone="N", volume="7642", speed=6).
    *   `Sound 2 (Kairos Chime)`: Resonant low celestial chord announcing circuit breaker (`c2g2c3`, tone="S", volume="6543", speed=15).
    *   `Sound 3 (Death Shatter)`: Extended descending noise burst simulating bursting glass (`g2d2a1d1`, tone="N", volume="7764", speed=12).
*   **Code Organization Blueprint:**
    *   `main.py`: Entrypoint, initialization, game state coordinator.
    *   `engine/state.py`: `GameState` enumeration (`TITLE`, `CHRONOS`, `KAIROS`, `GAMEOVER`) and state transitions.
    *   `engine/entities.py`: `HourglassPlayer`, `SandGrain`, `GlassShard`, `Particle` classes.
    *   `engine/bargains.py`: Faustian Bargain registry, deck shuffler, compounding penalty calculators.
    *   `engine/audio.py`: Procedural synth sound channel assignments.

## 07. Autonomous Agent Execution Loop & Validation Gates

**Fail-Loud Implementation Mandate:** The executing AI agent must construct the codebase systematically and enforce automated validation gates without waiting for human intervention.

*   **Virtual Environment Setup:**
    ```bash
    uv venv .venv
    source .venv/bin/activate
    uv pip install pyxel pytest
    ```
*   **Resourcefulness & Self-Healing Protocol:**
    *   If Pyxel GUI fails due to missing display server in headless testing: Use headless mock testing or execute with dummy display (`SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy python -m pytest`).
    *   Never halt on procedural errors. Read tracebacks, correct code directly, re-run tests.
*   **Programmatic Validation Gates (Automated Test Suite):**
    The agent must generate and pass a suite of unit tests (`tests/test_mechanics.py`):
    1.  *Chronos Descent & Hazard Gate:* Assert continuous downward descent ($V_y > 0$), lateral steering bounds, and collision with glass shards depleting hearts to terminate game when hearts reach 0.
    2.  *Kairos Timing Gate:* Assert Kairos triggers exactly at 8.0s ($240$ frames), freezes kinematics ($V_y = 0$), and times out or transitions upon input.
    3.  *Compounding Math Gate:* Verify bargain $Boon(k)$ strictly decreases and $Curse(k)$ strictly increases monotonically for selections $k \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$.
    4.  *Greed Deterministic Kill-Timer Gate:* Verify Sands of Greed initializes deterministic kill-timer terminating game upon expiration.
    5.  *Vignette Boundary Gate:* Assert $R_{vignette} > 0$ and clamps cleanly without rendering exceptions.

## 08. Packaging & Emscripten Web Distribution Pipeline

**Standalone Binary & Web Deployment:** Final artifacts must be bundled for seamless evaluation and web browser execution.

*   **Step 1: Standalone Package Assembly:**
    Run Pyxel packager to bundle code and synthesized assets into a standalone binary:
    ```bash
    pyxel package . main.py
    # Output: grain_of_doubt.pyxapp
    ```
*   **Step 2: WebAssembly HTML Export:**
    Compile the `.pyxapp` into self-contained HTML5/Emscripten bundle for zero-install browser execution:
    ```bash
    pyxel app2html grain_of_doubt.pyxapp
    # Output: grain_of_doubt.html
    ```
*   **Step 3: Web Verification:**
    Rename or link `grain_of_doubt.html` to `index.html` in root directory for automated GitHub Pages hosting via `pyxel.js`. Verify execution locally with `python -m http.server 8000`.
