## 02. Narrative Premise & 16-Color Visual Topology

**Falling Hourglass Endless Runner:** A 2D retro arcade game where the player steers a fragile falling hourglass down the cosmic-scale neck of a broken hourglass, dodging lethal glass shards, collecting golden sand to survive, and choosing high-stakes (Faustian) bargains that pause time for instant boosts with permanent penalties.

### Core Game Specification
*   **Genre:** 2D Retro Arcade Endless Runner (hyper-casual single-axis controls, SkiFree-style infinite horizontal arena).
*   **Author:** Arian Prabowo.
*   **Theme:** "Borrowed Time" (PyWeek 42).
*   **Title:** *Grain of Doubt*.
*   **Version:** v0.19.0.
*   **Visual Motif (Hourglass-ception):** A fragile player hourglass falling through the neck of a colossal broken cosmic hourglass. This nested framing directly dictates gameplay: collecting stray cascading sand (points) while dodging lethal glass shards (broken fragments of the outer hourglass).
*   **Core Traversal Loop:** *SkiFree*-style downward descent with zero stopping and an **infinitely wide horizontal arena** (player steers laterally $x \in (-\infty, +\infty)$ with horizontal camera tracking and a $\pm 4.5$ screen procedural generation horizon).
*   **Mechanics:** Dual-Phase Descent & Bargains
    *   *Chronos time:* Normal play is collecting sands and avoiding glass shards. Diegetic background void stars and collapsing cosmic hourglass walls provide dynamic temporal stimuli indicating progression toward Kairos (no HUD progress bar).
    *   *Kairos time:* Periodic time-freezes force the player to choose between exactly 2 Faustian bargains within 2.0s (failure to choose causes instant death by doubt paralysis).
    *   *Faustian Bargains:* Named directly after the Seven Deadly Sins in Catholic Gregorian order (**Pride**, **Greed**, **Lust**, **Envy**, **Gluttony**, **Wrath**, **Sloth**). Every deal has an instant boon and permanent compounding curse. Chosen strictly via `A`/`Left Arrow` (Left card) or `D`/`Right Arrow` (Right card).

### What is Borrowed Time? A Thematic Exploration

**Archetypes & Real-World Parallels:** "Borrowed time" is the precarious state of continuing to function after an inevitable fatal condition has been established, surviving strictly on deferrals and compounding interest.

*   **Related Concepts to "Borrowed Time":**
    * *Terminal Deferral:* Existing past an inescapable death sentence, terminal diagnosis, or structural insolvency. The subject is never saved; they are merely awaiting the postponed execution of the debt.
    * *Linear Flow vs. Opportune Ruptures (*Chronos* vs. *Kairos*):* In Greek thought, *Chronos* is the relentless, quantitative sequence of clock time devouring all creation. *Kairos* is the qualitative, opportune rupture—a fleeting opening where normal time freezes and a critical decision can alter immediate destiny. The player cannot stop Chronos; they can only steal desperate Kairos reprieves.
    * *The Sword of Damocles:* Enjoying supreme luxury and fortune while a razor-sharp sword hangs overhead by a single horsehair. Doom is certain, but the exact moment of severance is concealed.
    * *Faustian Bargain:* A pact with the Devil in exchange for temporary worldly power, youth, or knowledge. A Faustian bargain does not solve mortality—it trades long-term viability for immediate relief, living on borrowed grace while the debt accumulates.

### Thematic & Mechanical Design Decisions

**Grounded Creative Rationale:** Every visual element, traversal vector, and game rule directly translates the existential premise of "Borrowed Time" into playable systems without arbitrary video game tropes.

*   **Visual Motif: Why "Hourglass-ception"?**
    *   *Diegetic Asset Ecology:* Nesting a player hourglass inside the neck of a shattered cosmic hourglass provides an organic justification for all game entities: collectible points are stray falling sand grains, while lethal obstacles are falling glass shards from the outer shell.
    *   *Cosmic Recursion:* A fragile micro-vessel tumbling down a macro-vessel visualizes existential entrapment—individual mortal existence trapped within the larger entropic collapse of universal time.

*   **Traversal Vector: Why Downward Descent with Zero Stopping?**
    *   *Physical & Metaphorical Entropy:* Traditional runners move forward toward an open horizon, suggesting progress. Sand and gravity only move downward. Descent embodies collapse and loss of control—what goes up must come down.
    *   *Unforgiving Clock:* Time never stops or rewinds. Removing the ability to halt subjects the player to relentless gravitational pull toward terminal impact, able only to steer laterally across oncoming danger.

*   **Core Mechanics: Why Time-Freezes and Mandatory Cursed Deals?**
    *   *Dual-Pacing Tension (Chronos vs. Kairos):* Continuous dodging alone is merely a reflex test. Freezing the frantic descent for a brief decision window shifts the experience from kinetic reaction to psychological agony, forcing tactical reflection amidst chaos.
    *   *Borrowed Time as Predatory Debt:* Power-ups are not free gifts. True to the theme of borrowed time, every deal is mandatory and pairs immediate survival (a pro) with permanent structural decay (a con). Like a predatory loan, taking a deal buys immediate reprieve today by mortgaging future survival.

*   **Title: Why "Grain of Doubt"?**
    *   *Literal Grains of Sand:* "Grain" refers directly to the grains of sand inside the hourglass—the discrete physical units of time and collectible score cascading down the void.
    *   *The English Idiom:* "A grain of doubt" is an established English idiom meaning even the smallest trace of uncertainty that undermines complete confidence.
    *   *Kairos Decision Agony:* In gameplay, this captures the paralyzing doubt players experience during Kairos time-freezes. When forced to choose between 2 mandatory Faustian bargains, the player agonizes over trade-offs: *Which curse is survivable? Is the immediate boost worth future ruin?* That psychological hesitation—a single grain of doubt—is what shatters the run.

## 03. Dual-Clock Temporal Engine (Chronos vs. Kairos)

**Bifurcated Temporal State Architecture:** The game loop rejects singular linear time, implementing an explicit state machine oscillating between continuous relentless motion (**Chronos**) and frozen decision crossroads (**Kairos**).

*   **Temporal State Diagram:**
    ```
    [INIT] -> [TITLE_SCREEN] -> [CHRONOS_DESCENT (8.0s)] <----> [KAIROS_CIRCUIT_BREAKER (2.0s)]
                                      |                                   |
                                      +---------> [GAME_OVER] <-----------+
    ```
*   **State 1: Chronos Phase (Relentless Hypervigilance):**
    *   Duration: Fixed 8.0 seconds ($240$ frames at 30 FPS).
    *   Continuous Descent: Player descends vertically through void. Global vertical scroll speed $V_y$ streams glass shards and golden sand motes upward relative to camera.
    *   Score Collection: Collecting golden sand grains increments accumulated score ($Score \leftarrow Score + (\text{Points} \times M_{pts})$).
    *   Lethal Threat: Colliding with falling glass shards inflicts damage ($-1$ Heart out of initial 5). Depleting all 5 hearts causes catastrophic shatter and instant Game Over.
    *   Diegetic Temporal Feedback: Background void stars accelerate downward and transition through 4 chromatic phases (Blue $\to$ Cyan $\to$ Amber $\to$ Pulsing Red/Gold) to indicate time elapsed before the next Kairos rupture.
*   **State 2: Kairos Phase (Acute Panic Circuit Breaker):**
    *   Trigger: Automatically halts Chronos every 8.0 elapsed seconds.
    *   Duration: 2.0 real-time seconds ($60$ frames at 30 FPS).
    *   Circuit Breaker Mechanics: Kinematic velocities freeze ($V_y = 0$, player motion locked). Background void dims as searing geometric cards illuminate the display.
    *   UI Modal: System presents exactly 2 randomly drawn Faustian Bargain cards from the Seven Deadly Sins pool displayed across 2 wide spatial columns (Left vs. Right).
    *   Vertical Side Timers: Searing vertical gauge bars drain from top to bottom along the screen sides ($x=24$ and $x=570$).
    *   Player Input & Doubt Paralysis: Pressing `A` / `Left Arrow` selects Left; `D` / `Right Arrow` selects Right. Double-tapping the selected direction or pressing `Space`/`Enter` confirms immediately. **If the player fails to choose within 2.0 seconds, doubt paralyzes the vessel and it instantly shatters (*Paralyzed by Doubt: Kairos Expired*)**.

## 04. Faustian Bargain Mechanics & Mathematical Decay Curves

**Systems Architecture (Variation A: Mechanical):** Every bargain pairs immediate survival boons with compounding structural penalties, mapping the Seven Deadly Sins to mechanical trade-offs in **Gregorian / Catholic Canonical Order**.

*   **Seven Deadly Sins Bargain Registry (Catholic Canonical Order):**
    *   **Pride:**
        *   *Boon:* Spawns golden sand in organic randomized clusters (+1 grain per pact level: pairs = 2 grains on 1st pact, triplets = 3 on 2nd, quadruplets = 4 on 3rd). Clusters share trajectory with randomized non-overlapping spatial offsets ($r \in [14, 36]$ px; 1 normal sand = 1 point).
        *   *Penalty:* Increases global descent velocity multiplier by flat $+25\%$ per pact level ($+0.25$). Entity wave spawn accumulation scales proportionally with speed multiplier to preserve constant spatial hazard density down the void.
    *   **Greed:**
        *   *Boon:* Converts all accrued sand collections and shard dodges into an instant score bounty: $(N_{sand} + N_{shards}) \times 5.0 \times 0.75^k$. During Borrowed Time, collecting sand **multiplies current score by 110%** ($Score \leftarrow \max(Score + 1, \lfloor Score \times 1.10 \rfloor)$) instead of adding 1 point!
        *   *Penalty:* Triggers **Borrowed Time** for a randomized window between 10.0 and 18.0 seconds ($300$ to $540$ frames). Void erupts into blood crimson with ember stars (or blood river in sandfall terrain), descent velocity increases by $+20\% \times 1.5^k$, and spawn density increases by $+25\% \times 1.5^k$. **When Borrowed Time expires, the debt is collected and the player instantly dies (*"BORROWED TIME EXPIRED (DEBT COLLECTED)"*)**.
    *   **Lust:**
        *   *Boon:* Activates permanent magnetic attraction field pulling in golden sand ($r = 100.0 + k \times 50.0$ px) with second-order Newtonian momentum and viscous damping ($0.94$).
        *   *Penalty:* Establishes permanent magnetic attraction field pulling in lethal glass shards ($r = 100.0 + k \times 50.0$ px) with Newtonian momentum and viscous damping ($0.94$).
    *   **Envy:**
        *   *Boon:* **Tidal Pull:** For 2.0 seconds (60 frames), all golden sand grains within $2\times$ the current vignette pixel radius ($2 \times R_{\text{vignette}}$, $1228.8$px on Pact 1) are strongly attracted toward the hourglass with full Newtonian momentum.
        *   *Penalty:* Inflicts **Vignette Vision**, restricting visual range with a 5-tier graduated dither mask using recalibrated starting point $k_{\text{eff}} = k + 2$:
            * Outermost circle (zero vision beyond): $R_{\text{outer}} = 1200 \times 0.8^{k+2}$ px (Pact 1: $614.4$ px, Pact 2: $491.5$ px, Pact 3: $393.2$ px, Pact 4: $314.6$ px, Pact 5: $251.7$ px).
            * Innermost circle (full clear vision): $R_{\text{inner}} = 1000 \times 0.8^{k+3}$ px (Pact 1: $409.6$ px, Pact 2: $327.7$ px, Pact 3: $262.1$ px, Pact 4: $209.7$ px, Pact 5: $167.8$ px).
    *   **Gluttony:**
        *   *Boon:* **Fat Sand Grains:** At Gluttony level $N$, normal entity chance is $0.9^N$ and fat entity chance is $1.0 - 0.9^N$ (Level 0: 0% fat, 100% normal). Fat sand grains are $\sim 3\times$ larger linear dimension ($10\times$ area, $12\times 12$ vs $4\times 4$ px) and award $3\times$ points ($3$ points per grain).
        *   *Penalty:* **Fat Glass Shards:** With the same probability $1.0 - 0.9^N$, glass shards spawn as massive fat hazards ($\sim 2.8\times$ linear dimension, $10\times$ area, $30\times 45$ vs $20\times 30$ px hitbox). Additionally, in the Kairos modal, the card frame visibly bulges and bends outward by $+22$px around the title `GLUTTONY`.
    *   **Wrath:**
        *   *Boon:* **Wrath Blast:** Generates a massive radial kinetic shockwave centered at the player. Everything (both golden sand grains and glass shards) within a $1200$px radius is blasted outward with a smooth multi-frame acceleration curve ($\text{impulse} \le 46.0$ px/frame) and dramatic $20$-frame screen shake (12px intensity). Zero particle debris clutter.
        *   *Penalty:* Enforces zero-yield state (0 points per sand) for a flat 10.0 seconds ($300$ frames). Does not compound.
    *   **Sloth:**
        *   *Boon:* **Lazy Reprieve:** Sloth means lazy; lazy means doing nothing! All glass shards below the player within 3 screens wide are hurled downward to the bottom horizon ($v_y \ge 38.0$ px/frame, pushed to $y \ge y_{\text{player}} + 520$ px) over a smooth multi-frame burst acceleration curve. For ~2.0 to 2.5 seconds, the entire descent space below you is completely cleared of hazards, allowing you to literally do nothing and survive.
        *   *Penalty:* **Double Danger Wave:** Oncoming hazards seeded at bottom horizon ($y \in [1050, 1450]$ px) overlap with hurled shards into a dense wall of glass that scrolls back upward. Additionally, imposes permanent compounding lateral drag on hourglass steering: $-20\% \times 1.5^k$ (Pact 1: $-0.20$, Pact 2: $-0.30$, Pact 3: $-0.45$; minimum modifier $0.20$).

*   **Compounding Penalty & Diminishing Return Equations:**
    *   Let $k_i \in \mathbb{N}_0$ represent the number of times bargain $i$ has been selected.
    *   Boon scaling: $Boon_i(k_i) = Boon_{i, 0} \times (0.75)^{k_i}$
    *   Curse scaling: $Curse_i(k_i) = Curse_{i, 0} \times (1.50)^{k_i}$
    *   *Invariant Assertion:* Re-selecting identical sins exponentially accelerates player demise.

## 05. Kinematic Controls, Hitboxes & Particle Physics

**Downhill Traversal Engine:** Motion echoes classic downhill vertical runners (SkiFree) inverted into cosmic celestial void descent, upgraded with silky inertia gliding and infinite lateral roaming.

*   **Control Mapping:**
    *   `Left Arrow` / `Key A`: Accelerate lateral left vector ($-\hat{i}$). Also moves Kairos card selection left.
    *   `Right Arrow` / `Key D`: Accelerate lateral right vector ($+\hat{i}$). Also moves Kairos card selection right.
    *   *(Single-Axis Navigation Mandate: No aero-brake, no deep dive. Descent velocity is fixed).*
    *   `Space` / `Enter`: Start run from title screen; confirm Kairos card instantly; restart game after post-mortem lockout.
    *   `X`: In gameplay (Chronos/Kairos/Game Over), returns to Title Screen without quitting. On Title Screen, quits application (graceful no-op in web browser).
    *   `~` / `` ` `` (Backtick): Toggle Developer Mode overlay and live telemetry.
    *   `,` (Comma, in Dev Mode): Cycle backwards through all 20 divergent aesthetic themes.
    *   `.` (Period, in Dev Mode): Cycle forward through all 20 divergent aesthetic themes.
    *   `I` (in Dev Mode): Toggle God Mode (invulnerability to glass hazards and void collisions).
    *   `B` (in Dev Mode): Toggle autonomous GOFAI playtesting bot.
    *   `V` (in Dev Mode): Toggle lossless FFmpeg canvas video recorder (`.mp4`).
    *   `1` - `7` (in Dev Mode): Add Faustian pact level (1:Pride, 2:Greed, 3:Lust, 4:Envy, 5:Gluttony, 6:Wrath, 7:Sloth).
    *   `Q, W, E, R, T, Y, U` (in Dev Mode): Decrement Faustian pact level and relax active modifiers.
    *   *Mobile Touch Controls:* Context-sensitive `< LEFT` ($x=30$) and `RIGHT >` ($x=320$) arcade buttons rendered when `is_mobile` with dither alpha (`0.30` idle, `0.60` pressed). Tapping screen halves also steers laterally.
    *   *Kairos Re-press Protection:* Entering Kairos requires unpressing/releasing steering first before selecting a card, preventing accidental choices when holding arrows across transitions.
    *   *Post-Mortem Input Lockout:* A mandatory 2.0-second (60-frame) debounce lockout prevents accidental restarts upon game over.

*   **Kinematic State Vector & Silky Gliding Inertia:**
    *   Player Position: $\mathbf{P} = (x, y)$, where horizontal coordinate $x \in (-\infty, +\infty)$ with horizontal camera tracking $cam_x = int(x - 300)$.
    *   Horizontal Velocity: $v_x(t + 1) = (v_x(t) + a_x) \times \mu_x$, where silky damping friction $\mu_x = 0.94$ (air friction coefficient $0.06$) and baseline acceleration $a_x = 2.4$.
    *   *Terminal Velocity:* $v_{x, max} = \frac{a_x \cdot \mu_x}{1 - \mu_x} = \frac{2.4 \times 0.94}{0.06} \approx 37.6\text{ px/frame}$.
    *   *Infinite Arena & Spawn Margin:* Procedural wave generation spans $\pm 2700\text{ px}$ ($\pm 4.5$ screens) centered on player ($[x - 2700, x + 2700]$), ensuring continuous hazard populating during sustained lateral flight. Despawn culling boundary is set to $3500\text{ px}$ laterally.

*   **Deep World Simulation (10 Seconds Down / Consequence Catch-up):**
    *   Downhill simulation domain extends to $y \le 6000.0$ pixels (~10–12 seconds of downward descent). Downward-hurled hazards (Sloth Lazy Reprieve) and blasted entities (Wrath blast) persist in physics simulation, retain velocity up to $50.0$ px/frame, and eventually catch back up as the player continues downward.

*   **Entity Hitbox & Motion Topology ($600 \times 800$ Resolution):**
    *   Player Hourglass: Centered horizontal AABB $60 \times 40$ pixels, tilting dynamically with lateral velocity $v_x$ ($\pm 22^\circ$). Zero particle popups; internal bulbs and waist flash in radiant gold upon sand collection.
    *   Sand Grains: Normal $4 \times 4$ px glistening motes (collection hitbox $30 \times 30$ px; $+1$ point). Fat Sand Grains (Gluttony): $12 \times 12$ px ($10\times$ area; $+3$ points). Dynamic drift $\in [-0.4, 0.4]$ px/frame plus sinusoidal shimmer drift.
    *   Glass Shards: Procedurally generated acute, scalene, and obtuse non-right-angled triangles. Continuous angular spin $\text{spin\_speed} \in [-0.18, -0.04] \cup [0.04, 0.18]$ rad/frame coupled aerodynamically to horizontal airspeed $V_x$. Collision hitbox $20 \times 30$ px (Fat Shards: $30 \times 45$ px, $10\times$ area). Effect: $-1$ Heart (out of 5), screen shake (12px, 8 frames), 0.66s invulnerability flash.

## 09. Concrete Implementation Decisions & TGDD Architecture

### 9.1 Architectural Modularization Strategy
To guarantee headless validation under CI/CLI without an SDL2 video driver while preserving high-performance Pyxel rendering, the codebase is strictly decoupled into a headless model layer and a Pyxel view/controller layer:
1. **Model Layer (`engine/`):**
   * Completely independent of `pyxel` module imports at runtime.
   * `StateManager` handles numeric time, counters, multipliers, 5 initial life points, lifetime frame metrics, and death taxonomies.
   * `EntityManager` executes pure coordinate transformations, infinite horizontal coordinates, vector damping, lateral drift/oscillations, particle lifespans, and collision geometries.
   * `BargainManager` executes RNG deck shuffles (drawing 2 options) and decay curves with zero GUI dependencies.
   * `AudioManager` encapsulates sound playback in safe try-catch blocks with graceful fallbacks.
2. **View & Controller Layer (`main.py`):**
   * Integrates hardware input (`pyxel.btn`, `pyxel.btnp`), frame buffers, SkiFree horizontal camera tracking, and sound synthesis.
   * Encapsulates `GrainOfDoubtApp` which can be instantiated with `headless=True` for automated continuous simulation.

### 9.2 Kinematic Traversal & SkiFree Infinite Arena Mapping
In downhill endless runners (SkiFree), the player travels downwards into an unbounded world. In retro 2D screen coordinates, $y=0$ is the top of the canvas and $y=800$ is the bottom:
* **Vertical Aspect Ratio Architecture ($600 \times 800$ Pixels, $3:4$ Portrait Viewport):**
  * Resolution expanded to $600 \times 800$ to provide expansive retro-arcade fidelity, smooth rendering, and ample forward lookahead down the void.
  * Captures the vertical plunge of descending through the cosmic hourglass void.
* **SkiFree Dynamic Camera Tracking:**
  * Horizontal camera tracking locks to the player: `cam_x = int(player.x - SCREEN_WIDTH / 2.0)`.
  * Pyxel viewport camera is updated dynamically: `pyxel.camera(cam_x + shake_ox, shake_oy)`.
* **Opposing World Vector:** World entities (sand motes, cosmic shards, astral dust) stream **upwards** relative to screen coordinates ($y \leftarrow y - V_y$).
* **Single-Axis Navigation Mandate (No Dive, No Aero-Brake):**
  * Vertical descent speed is fixed ($V_{y, 0} = 10.0$ px/frame, modified only by global pace multipliers from bargains like Pride).
  * Player vertical coordinate remains locked at $y = 200.0$.
* **Infinite Lateral Traversal Parameters & Silky Gliding:**
  * Baseline lateral acceleration: $a_x = 2.4$ px/$\text{frame}^2$.
  * Viscous damping coefficient: $\mu_x = 0.94$ (air friction coefficient $0.06$).
  * Terminal velocity: $v_{x, terminal} = \frac{a_x \cdot \mu_x}{1 - \mu_x} = \frac{2.4 \times 0.94}{0.06} \approx 37.6$ px/frame ($\approx 1128.0$ px/s at 30 FPS).
  * Boundary clamping: **Completely unbounded** ($x \in (-\infty, +\infty)$). No hard side walls blocking steering. Player roams infinitely across the horizontal horizon with camera tracking.

### 9.3 Spawning & Deep Entity Lifecycle Dynamics
* **Accumulator Pattern with Scaled Spatial Density:**
  * Rather than integer modulus checks, spawning uses a fractional float accumulator halved by 50% to prevent visual clutter:
    $$Acc(t+1) = Acc(t) + (M_{spawn} \times 0.50 \times M_{speed})$$
    Scaling directly with $M_{speed}$ guarantees that when descent speed increases (e.g., via Pride), spatial entity density (entities per 100 vertical pixels fallen) remains perfectly constant rather than diluting. While $Acc \ge 1.0$, one entity wave spawns and $Acc \leftarrow Acc - 1.0$.
* **Entity Distribution Ratio:** Each spawned wave allocates a 60% probability for collectible sand motes (`SandGrain`) and a 40% probability for lethal hazards (`GlassShard`).
* **Entity Lateral Dynamics:** Grains and shards feature small lateral X-axis drift and flutter oscillations, ensuring natural falling chaos rather than rigid vertical tracks.
* **Procedural Horizon Wave Generation ($1300$ px Horizon):**
  * Entities are generated procedurally across a wide horizon surrounding the camera viewport:
    $$x_{spawn} \in [cam\_x - 350.0, cam\_x + 600.0 + 350.0]$$
    This provides a seamless 350px off-screen buffer on both sides, ensuring smooth lateral scrolling without popping.
  * Vertical spawn coordinate: $y_{spawn} \in [820.0, 900.0]$.
* **Deep World Simulation (10 Seconds Down / Consequence Catch-up):**
  * Downhill simulation domain extends downward to $y \le 6000.0$ pixels (~10–12 seconds of downward descent). Downward-hurled hazards (Sloth Lazy Reprieve) and blasted entities (Wrath blast) persist in physics simulation, retain velocity up to $50.0$ px/frame, and eventually catch back up as the player continues downward.
* **Despawn & Bypassed Pool:**
  * Entities passing $y < -40.0$ (sand) or $y < -60.0$ (shards) are marked dead.
  * Entities drifting laterally past $|x - player.x| > 3500.0$ are culled from memory.
  * Uncollected sand crossing $y < -40.0$ increments `bypassed_sand_pool`, tracked for retroactive harvesting via the Envy pact.
  * Dodged shards crossing $y < -60.0$ increment the run metric `total_shards_dodged`.

### 11.6 Scanline Double-Ring Vignette Mask Algorithm
Rendered in fixed screen coordinates (`pyxel.camera(0, 0)`) centered at $(300, 200)$ to prevent camera-offset artifacts. Uses a two-stage scanline rasterizer:
1. Inner ring ($R_{inner} = R_{vignette}$): 50% checkerboard dither (`(x + y) % 2 == 0`) between $R_{inner}$ and $R_{outer}$.
2. Outer ring ($R_{outer} = R_{vignette} \times 1.28$): 100% solid pitch black (`Color 0`).
```python
def render_vignette(px, py, radius, screen_w=600, screen_h=800, pyxel_module=pyxel):
    r_in = max(15.0, radius)
    r_out = max(20.0, radius * 1.28)
    r_in_sq = r_in * r_in
    r_out_sq = r_out * r_out
    for y in range(screen_h):
        dy = y - py
        dy_sq = dy * dy
        if dy_sq >= r_out_sq:
            pyxel_module.rect(0, y, screen_w, 1, 0)
        else:
            dx_out = math.sqrt(r_out_sq - dy_sq)
            dx_in = math.sqrt(r_in_sq - dy_sq) if dy_sq < r_in_sq else 0.0
            # Solid black outer masks
            lx_out = int(px - dx_out)
            rx_out = int(px + dx_out)
            if lx_out > 0:
                pyxel_module.rect(0, y, lx_out, 1, 0)
            if rx_out < screen_w:
                pyxel_module.rect(rx_out, y, screen_w - rx_out, 1, 0)
            # 50% dither transition zone between r_in and r_out
            lx_in = int(px - dx_in)
            rx_in = int(px + dx_in)
            for x in range(max(0, lx_out), min(screen_w, lx_in)):
                if (x + y) % 2 == 0:
                    pyxel_module.pset(x, y, 0)
            for x in range(max(0, rx_in), min(screen_w, rx_out)):
                if (x + y) % 2 == 0:
                    pyxel_module.pset(x, y, 0)
```
* Computational Complexity: $\mathcal{O}(H)$ where $H = 800$. Requires native C line rects plus bounded scanline dither points, executing well within $<0.4$ ms per frame. Initialized at $R_0 = 1000.0$ px.

## 14. Production Packaging, Deployment & Web Verification Specs

### 14.1 Standalone Package Assembly (`.pyxapp`)
Pyxel packages application scripts and assets into a portable zip-compressed binary container:
```bash
# Clean previous artifacts to prevent recursive archive pollution
rm -f *.pyxapp *.html
# Package application
pyxel package . main.py
# Standardize distribution name
mv *.pyxapp grain_of_doubt.pyxapp
```
* Container structure: includes `.pyxapp_startup_script` pointing to `main.py`, `engine/` package modules, and documentation files.

### 14.2 WebAssembly / Emscripten HTML5 Export (`index.html`)
The standalone package is compiled into a client-side WebAssembly distribution:
```bash
pyxel app2html grain_of_doubt.pyxapp
```
* **HTML Post-Processing & Optimization (`optimize_web.py`):**
  Pyxel's standard `app2html` output is optimized by running `python optimize_web.py`:
  1. Replaces default cross gamepad with customized 2-button touch layout.
  2. Injects pure CSS responsive containment (`--target-w: min(var(--avail-w), calc(var(--avail-h) * 0.75))`) and `canvas { object-fit: contain; }` to guarantee letterboxing on all mobile screens and desktop monitors without aspect-ratio distortion.
  3. Injects multi-layered mobile browser zoom prevention:
     - `touch-action: none !important`, `overscroll-behavior: none !important`, `-webkit-touch-callout: none !important`, `-webkit-user-select: none !important`.
     - JavaScript event interception canceling Safari `gesturestart`/`change`/`end`, multi-touch pinch `touchstart`/`touchmove` ($>1$ touch), double-tap debounce ($<300$ms), and Ctrl-wheel trackpad zoom.
  4. Synchronizes both `grain_of_doubt.html` and `index.html` for GitHub Pages hosting.

### 14.3 Official PyWeek 42 Distribution Zip (`build.sh`)
The master build script (`./build.sh`) automates the complete test, packaging, and distribution pipeline:
```bash
./build.sh
```
* Execution steps:
  1. Runs full 60-test automated test suite (`.venv/bin/pytest tests/ -v`).
  2. Cleans previous bundle artifacts.
  3. Builds standalone `grain_of_doubt.pyxapp`.
  4. Exports WebAssembly `grain_of_doubt.html`.
  5. Optimizes HTML with `optimize_web.py` and copies to `index.html`.
  6. Packages official PyWeek entry archive `grain-of-doubt-0.19.0.zip` containing all executable code (`main.py`, `run_game.py`), the `engine/` package, `tests/`, requirements, documentation (`README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `p03.md`, `LICENSE.md`), and launchers.
